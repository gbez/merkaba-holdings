## What you'll do:

- **Source deals:** Research industries and build target lists of privately held companies that fit our acquisition criteria, using databases, trade associations, and public records.
- **Reach out to business owners:** Draft and send personalized emails and letters, make introductory calls, and track every conversation in our CRM.&#32;
  - For highly personalized outreach efforts, I will lend you my 1929 Royal typewriter and teach you how to seal envelopes with wax.&#32;
- **Cover the broker market:** Monitor listing sites and broker newsletters, request information on promising deals, and screen them against our criteria.
- **Research industries:** Write short industry memos on market size, competition, growth drivers, risks, and typical valuations.
- **Analyze companies:** Review financials and confidential information memoranda, calculate adjusted EBITDA, build simple valuation and debt-service models, and write one-page deal summaries.
- **Support diligence:** On deals that reach letter-of-intent stage, help with customer and competitor calls, data-room organization, and analysis of customers, pricing, and margins.
- **Contribute to investor materials:** Help prepare investor updates and deal presentations.

## Who I'm looking for:

- Current MBA or undergraduate students with a strong interest in entrepreneurship, private equity, and/or small-business ownership.
- Comfortable with Excel and basic financial statements; accounting or finance coursework is a plus.
- Clear, professional writer who can craft outreach that gets responses.
- Confident on the phone and genuinely curious about how businesses work.
- Self-directed, organized, and reliable with minimal supervision.
- Resilient: most outreach goes unanswered, and that's part of the job.
- Discreet with confidential information.

**Nice to have:** experience in sales, consulting, banking, or operating roles; familiarity with CRMs or company databases; membership in an ETA or search fund club.

## What you'll get:

- A front-row seat to a live search, from sourcing through negotiation, and a practical playbook if you're considering your own search one day.
- Direct mentorship, with regular one-on-ones and deal debriefs.
- **Real responsibility and work you can speak to in interviews.**
- Exposure to my network of investors, advisors, and lenders.
- A strong reference and, for standout interns, the possibility of a role post-acquisition.
