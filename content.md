# Merkaba Holdings — Website Text

Edit the text below, save this file, then run this from a terminal in this
folder to update `index.html`:

```
python3 build.py
```

A few rules so the script can find its way back to the right spot on the page:

- **Don't rename the `##` or `###` headings.** They're how the script matches
  each block back to its spot in `index.html`. Renaming one just means that
  one field won't update (you'll see a warning when you run the script) —
  nothing else breaks.
- **Do edit everything else freely** — the text under each heading, including
  the italicized description lines (those are just notes for you and are
  ignored by the script).
- Text in **[square brackets]** is a placeholder — replace it with your real
  information.
- Lines starting with `- ` are list items; keep that `- ` prefix on each line
  you want to keep as a separate item.

## Frequently Asked Questions
*Each question below becomes a clickable row that expands to show its answer.*

### Eyebrow
Questions

### Heading
Frequently asked questions

### Question 1
_The "Q:" line is the clickable question; the "A:" line (can wrap onto more lines) is the answer shown when it's expanded._
Q: What is a search fund?
A: A search fund is a way for an individual to find, buy, and personally run one established small business. Unlike a private equity firm, a searcher becomes the full-time CEO of the company they buy and typically plans to own it for many years.

### Question 2
Q: What does "self-funded" mean?
A: It means I'm funding my search myself instead of raising money from outside investors up front. For the purchase itself, I use a combination of SBA-backed bank loans, my own equity, and sometimes seller financing. Because I answer to no investment committee, I can be flexible on deal structure and move quickly.

### Question 3
Q: Will you keep my employees?
A: Yes. A business is only as strong as its people, and I'm buying yours because it already works. I plan to keep your team in place and invest in their growth.

### Question 4
Q: How long do I need to stay after the sale?
A: That's up to you. Most transitions last anywhere from a few months to a year, and we'll agree on the details together before closing.

### Question 5
Q: How is the purchase financed?
A: Most acquisitions I pursue are financed through an SBA 7(a) loan, my own equity investment, and in some cases a seller note. I'll explain the structure clearly in any offer so you know exactly how and when you'll be paid.

### Question 6
Q: Is our conversation confidential?
A: Absolutely. I'll never share that we've spoken, and I'm happy to sign an NDA before any business details are exchanged.
