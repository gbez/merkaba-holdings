How It Works

A simple, respectful process

I know you have a business to run. I'll keep things efficient and move at a pace that's comfortable for you.

  1. 1

    Introductory conversation

    An informal, confidential call to get to know each other and talk about your goals. No commitment and no paperwork.

  2. 2

    Learning about the business

    After signing an NDA, I'll review basic financials and ask questions so I can understand what makes your company special.

  5. 5

    Closing & transition

    We close the deal and begin the handoff together, following the transition plan we agreed on.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is a search fund?

A search fund is a way for an individual to find, buy, and personally run one established small business. Unlike a private equity firm, a searcher becomes the full-time CEO of the company they buy and typically plans to own it for many years.

What does "self-funded" mean?

It means I'm funding my search myself instead of raising money from outside investors up front. For the purchase itself, I use a combination of SBA-backed bank loans, my own equity, and sometimes seller financing. Because I answer to no investment committee, I can be flexible on deal structure and move quickly.

Will you keep my employees?

Yes. A business is only as strong as its people, and I'm buying yours because it already works. I plan to keep your team in place and invest in their growth.

How long do I need to stay after the sale?

That's up to you. Most transitions last anywhere from a few months to a year, and we'll agree on the details together before closing.

How is the purchase financed?

Most acquisitions I pursue are financed through an SBA 7(a) loan, my own equity investment, and in some cases a seller note. I'll explain the structure clearly in any offer so you know exactly how and when you'll be paid.

Is our conversation confidential?

Absolutely. I'll never share that we've spoken, and I'm happy to sign an NDA before any business details are exchanged.
